Test Call App

A purpose-built application for Customer Service employee testing and training, replacing a manual process previously managed across Google Forms, Excel, and SharePoint to create test scenarios, score individual calls against expected behaviors, and generate weekly results reporting.

Problem

The process was previously managed manually across Google Forms, Excel, and SharePoint, and maintaining it was a person's full-time job — hours spent daily just keeping the process running.

My Role

Built the application end-to-end: scenario creation and scheduling, the behavior-scoring workflow for reviewing each test call, and the weekly results reporting.

Impact

Adopted by Customer Service (3 admins + 12 testers), replacing the manual process that was disjointed across several forms and data sources. The app brought together the logging of test calls, the creation of testing scenarios, and the reporting / data sourcing for everything all in one place.
