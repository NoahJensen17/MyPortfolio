Pricing Tool - Pricing Analysis & Competition Review Tool

A full modernization of a legacy Microsoft Access application used by the Pricing department to enter and review competitor pricing data, maintain pricing records in bulk, and generate margin and competitive analysis reports.

Problem

The legacy Access-based tool was extremely slow and visually outdated, and lacked the ability to be enhanced with new features or additions.

My Role

Rebuilt the application end-to-end with a modernized UI, optimized runtime performance, recreated legacy reports as modern paginated reports, and added the ability for the tool to be managed without my direct involvement.

Impact

Adopted by the Pricing department and additional business users (20+ users), replacing a slow and rigid legacy system with a faster, more maintainable platform.
