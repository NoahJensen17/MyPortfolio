Church Reporting Tool

A serve team management and member onboarding tool built for my church. Replaces a manual process previously tracked through a mix of Google Forms and Google Sheets across individual teams by pulling together existing and third-party data sources into one place for reporting and onboarding insights.

Problem

Reporting and onboarding tracking for serve teams was done manually through a mix of Google Forms and Google Sheets, spread across each individual team.

My Role

Built the application end-to-end, including pulling in the organization's existing data sources plus a new third-party data source already used elsewhere for automatic tracking, to bring richer insights into one tool.

Impact

Used by a handful of key leaders to get quick insights and keep a heartbeat on their teams' status. Automates data gathering that was previously manual and surfaces more information than was accessible before, all in one convenient location.
